Logo direction
Since you liked the idea that name and mark don't have to literally match (like Kafka isn't a bird logo), here's what I'd suggest:

A single abstract wisp/curl — 2–3 curved lines rising and thinning out, like a minimal line-art smoke trace. Not a literal cartoon cloud — closer to a brush-stroke or an EKG-like rising curve, which doubles as "data trend line" (nice dual meaning: smoke and your stats évolution dans le temps chart).
Alternative: a small dot/spark at the base with one thin rising line — even more minimal, reads well at tiny sizes (favicon, PDF header).
Palette: warm ochre/amber for the wisp against dark ink-navy or charcoal background — feels "early warning" without going red-alert/hacker-cliché.
Typography: clean geometric sans-serif, lowercase "plume" reads modern and calm rather than aggressive.
